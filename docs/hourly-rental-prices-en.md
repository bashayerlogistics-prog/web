# Hourly Car Rental with Driver — Pricing (English)

**Service (AR):** استئجار سيارة بالسائق  
**Durations:** 4 / 8 / 12 hours  
**Cars:** Ford Taurus 2026 · Toyota Camry 2026 · Hyundai Staria 2026 · GMC 2026 · Toyota Hiace 2026  
**Currency:** SAR  
**Base cities:** Taif · Makkah · Jeddah · Madinah  

**Notes**
- **Internal trips** = trips within the city (from trip start time) — also see [within-city-trips-prices-en.md](./within-city-trips-prices-en.md)
- **Cross-city** = hours from arrival at destination with round trip (go & return)
- Package price is the **total** for the selected hours (not per-hour × hours only)
- **Index:** [README.md](./README.md)

## How it works in the app

1. **Forms:** Select **Hourly** → city (From) → destination → **4 / 8 / 12 hours** → car → Search  
2. **Result:** Only the **selected car** price for that hours + location  
3. **Superadmin:** `/admin/hourly` — import defaults, edit prices, active/inactive, hide price
